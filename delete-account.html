Delete your Road of Ashes account

Road of Ashes · Barcan Games · Effective 13 October 2026

You can delete your Road of Ashes online account and its data at any time, from inside the game or by email. You do not need to reinstall the game.

In the game

  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Support.
  2. Tick I understand: delete my online account.
  3. Tap Delete online account. Deletion happens right away.

The button needs an internet connection. If it is unavailable, use email instead.

By email

Write to razvan.barcan.br@gmail.com with the subject Road of Ashes account deletion. Send it from the email linked to your game profile, and include your in-game display name.

If no email is linked to your profile, tell us so and we will agree on another way to confirm the account is yours. We answer within 30 days and confirm when the deletion is complete.

Never send passwords, one-time codes or payment card details.

What is deleted

  • Your online profile: display name and avatar.
  • Leaderboard results and your arena defense build.
  • Arena battle history in which you were the attacker or the defender, and weekly arena points.
  • Your cosmetic collection: Cosmetic Sigils, unlocked cosmetics and purchase records.
  • Records of rewarded-ad sessions.
  • Your sign-in account, including any linked email address.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Your adventure save stays on your device. It never leaves the phone. Remove it from Settings in the game, or by uninstalling.
  • Google Play keeps its own records of your purchases under Google's policies. Deleting your game account does not refund purchases. Purchases cannot be moved to a different game account afterwards.
  • Our hosting provider's encrypted backups may keep deleted data for up to 30 days before they are overwritten. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.
