Privacy policy

Road of Ashes · Barcan Games · Effective 13 October 2026

Road of Ashes is a dark fantasy game for Android, made by Barcan Games, an independent developer based in Romania. This policy explains what the game processes, why, and the choices you have. Contact: razvan.barcan.br@gmail.com.

On your device only

Your adventure progress, settings, your age range and ad choices are saved on your phone. They are not sent to us.

Online features

The leaderboard, the arena and the cosmetic collection use an online account hosted by our provider, Supabase. Using them processes:

  • an account identifier created automatically for your device;
  • the display name and avatar you choose, which other players can see;
  • leaderboard results, your arena defense build and arena battle results;
  • an email address and one-time codes, only if you link an email for account recovery.

We use this to run these features, to let you recover your account, to answer support requests and to prevent abuse. The game works offline without them.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. To confirm what you own, restore it on another device and handle refunds, the game sends our server the purchase token and product name. Google receives a one-way hash of your game account identifier, so a purchase stays tied to your account. Purchases are cosmetic or skip the optional ads; none of them adds combat power.

Optional ads

The game never shows ads on its own. An ad plays only when you choose to watch one for a reward. Before your first ad, the game asks for your age range.

  • 18 or older: Google's consent form, where your region requires one, lets you choose whether ads may be personalized. You can change this in Privacy & Support → Ad privacy choices.
  • 16–17: ads are requested as age-restricted and never personalized.
  • Under 16: no ads are shown. Every part of the game remains playable.

When an ad is requested, Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform process data under Google's policies. This may include device and advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions and diagnostics. Non-personalized ads still involve this processing. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

For ads that grant Cosmetic Sigils, our server records a reward request identifier, your account identifier, a time and Google's verified transaction identifier. We use them to grant each reward once and to enforce the daily limit. Other ad rewards are granted on your device and are not recorded by us.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is processed by our service providers only to run the game: Supabase (hosting and accounts), Google Play (purchases) and Google AdMob (optional ads). Other players see your display name, avatar, leaderboard results and arena builds.

Security and retention

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Online data is kept while your account exists. Backups and logs are kept for at most 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Change or withdraw ad choices in Privacy & Support, or simply never watch an ad.
  • Delete your online account at any time: see account deletion.
  • Ask for a copy of your online data, or for corrections, by email. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, including complaining to your data protection authority.

Children

Road of Ashes is meant for players aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has created an online profile, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page when the game's data practices change, and show the new effective date above.
